**01.multibam_degs_promoter.sh**: Calculate the h3k27ac, h3k4me3 and h3k27me3 signals at the promoter regions of differentially expressed genes. 

**02.correction_plot_degs_promoter.ipynb**: Correct the calculated histone promoter signals. 

**01.scale_region_h3k27ac_mock_vs_tgev.sh**: Calculate the h3k27ac signals before and after infection separately. 

**02.profiler_h3k27ac_mock_vs_tgev.sh**: Draw a profiler graph of the h3k27ac signal before and after the infection calculation. 

**DEGs_cluster.R**: Draw a clustering diagram of differentially expressed genes. 

**DESeq2.R**: Differential gene analysis. 

**GO_Mock_VS_TGEV.R**: Functional enrichment analysis of differentially expressed genes. 

**h3k27ac_anno.r**: Annotation of H3K27AC peak. 

**kegg.R**: Enrichment analysis of KEGG pathways for all the differentially expressed genes.

**kegg_tree.r**: Cluster tree diagram of KEGG enrichment results for differentially expressed genes.

**motif_bubble_plot.R**: Drawing of the bubble chart for the analysis results of enhancer motifs before and after infection.

**rnaseq_hic_cor.R**: Correlation analysis of biological reproducibility of RNA-seq and BL-Hi-C.

**volcano_top10_markered.R**: Voronoi diagram of differentially expressed genes.
